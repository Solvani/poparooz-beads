# Poparooz Marketing Consent v1 MC-A00 Product, Data, and Privacy Contract

## 1. Authority and status

```text
Product: Poparooz Generator
Domain: Marketing Consent v1
Stage: MC-A00
Status: APPROVED / FROZEN
Implementation release: GRANTED
Runtime implementation: NOT STARTED at MC-A01
```

MC-A00 is the frozen product, data, and privacy authority for Marketing Consent
v1. MC-A01 synchronizes this authority into repository governance. Later stages
may implement and verify the runtime only within this contract.

This authority supersedes only the deferred Marketing Consent-specific
decisions in the historical Email Download Gate contract. It does not rewrite
that historical evidence, reopen Email Gate A08, or alter the frozen Email Gate
verification behavior.

## 2. Product boundary

Email verification remains required for Download. Marketing consent is
completely optional and must never be a prerequisite for:

- OTP issuance;
- OTP verification;
- local Email Gate unlock; or
- Pattern Download.

The consent control is a checkbox with these frozen properties:

```text
visible: true
required: false
defaultChecked: false
```

It appears only on the email-entry step and is never prechecked. Its exact
customer-visible copy is:

> Optional — Email me occasional Poparooz updates, pattern ideas, and offers. Unsubscribe anytime.

Changing the email starts an ungranted consent state. The OTP step does not
repeat the checkbox or ask for consent again.

## 3. Consent states and grant rule

The only product-level consent states are:

- `granted`: the Marketing Consent control was presented and the customer
  explicitly selected it;
- `declined`: the Marketing Consent control was presented but the customer did
  not grant it; and
- `not_present`: the Marketing Consent context or field was absent from that
  verification context, including an older client or flow where the context
  does not exist.

A grant must never be inferred from form submission, an OTP request, OTP
verification, Download, prior history, silence, or the absence of a value.

The effective grant rule is exactly:

```text
effectiveGrant = affirmativeIntent AND sameEmailVerified AND persistenceSucceeded
```

If marketing persistence fails, Marketing Consent remains false. Email Gate
verification and local unlock remain successful, and Download remains allowed.

## 4. Verification authority and email identity

Email Gate is the sole verification authority. Marketing Consent uses the same
canonical email verified by Email Gate and must not trust a re-supplied client
email.

Consent never transfers when the email changes. Re-grant after withdrawal, and
a grant for a different email, require new affirmative consent, trusted
same-email verification, and successful persistence.

## 5. Version and source

The frozen consent metadata is:

```text
consentVersion: marketing-consent-v1.0.0
sourceContext: generator_email_download_gate
```

`consentTimestamp` is generated by the server at successful persistence, which
is the moment the grant becomes effective. It is not the render, checkbox
click, email-entry, challenge, send-request, OTP-request, or OTP-only-success
time.

## 6. D1-first durable data contract

Marketing Consent v1 is D1-first and has two logical tables.

### `marketing_subscriptions`

The logical fields are:

- `subscription_id`;
- `canonical_email`;
- `status`;
- `consent_version`;
- `consent_source_context`;
- `consent_timestamp`;
- `withdrawn_timestamp`;
- `created_at`; and
- `updated_at`.

The only subscription statuses are `active` and `withdrawn`.
`canonical_email` is intended to be unique in this logical table.

### `marketing_consent_events`

The logical fields are exactly:

- `event_id`;
- `subscription_id`;
- `event_type`;
- `consent_version`;
- `source_context`; and
- `event_timestamp`.

Initial Marketing Consent v1 event types are `granted` and `withdrawn`. The
event table does not duplicate email when the subscription relationship
supplies it. It is not an analytics system or CRM.

Durable Marketing Consent rows are created only when affirmative consent and
trusted same-email verification have occurred and persistence succeeds. No
durable Marketing Consent row is created for `declined`, `not_present`, an
unverified, failed, or expired Email Gate flow, or a persistence failure. There
is no historical backfill.

## 7. Retention, withdrawal, and deletion

Email Gate retention remains unchanged.

An active subscription retains its canonical email while it is active.
Withdrawal makes marketing eligibility false immediately; withdrawal is not
the same as deletion. For V1, the target retention period for minimal withdrawn
suppression and audit data is 24 months.

Deletion should remove `canonical_email`, identifiable Marketing Consent
subscription data, identifiable consent history, and future provider copies if
such providers are later separately authorized. Exact deletion fields and
provider operations require later authorization. Nonidentifying aggregates may
remain after identifying data is removed.

## 8. Privacy and system isolation

Marketing Consent data is isolated from all user image, Pattern, PNG, material,
color, and image-metadata data. Those assets and computations remain
browser-local. Marketing Consent introduces no join to them, no upload of them,
and no customer account.

Marketing Consent v1 creates or changes no Shopify customer, Shopify customer
account, Shopify marketing status, Shopify subscription, or other Shopify data.

Marketing Consent v1 is D1-first only. It creates or changes no Resend Contacts,
Audiences, Topics, campaigns, Shopify
marketing record, CRM record, or other provider marketing record. Resend
remains transactional verification transport only.

Logs and diagnostics must not expose raw email where avoidable, OTPs, Turnstile
tokens, secrets, PII-bearing request bodies, or image, Pattern, or PNG content.
Any later marketing reporting must be aggregate-first and nonidentifying.

## 9. Ops Dashboard boundary

Ops Dashboard v1 remains on HOLD. This contract authorizes no dashboard API,
aggregate table, schema, code, deployment, or production mutation. No aggregate
schema is frozen here. Any future Ops Dashboard work requires separate design
and authorization.

## 10. Deliberately unfrozen implementation details

This contract intentionally does not freeze exact runtime routes, JSON shapes,
TypeScript types, class names, index names, migration names, or function names.
MC-A02 may choose the smallest implementation details consistent with this
frozen authority.
